How It Works
  1. Submit your application with your documents.
  2. Our team verifies your enrolment (within 24 h).
  3. On approval, your profile goes live on the directory.
  4. Add your available time slots to your dashboard.
  5. Students book and pay KES 100 — you receive KES 70.
  6. Conduct the 15-minute WhatsApp call at the booked time.
  7. Withdraw your earnings anytime via M-Pesa.
Our Verification Process

We take mentor quality seriously. Every application is reviewed by a human before approval — we do not auto-approve.

Our team cross-checks your student ID, portal screenshot, and (where provided) your institutional email to confirm active enrolment.

If additional information is needed, we may contact you directly. Approved mentors must maintain professional conduct or risk removal.
