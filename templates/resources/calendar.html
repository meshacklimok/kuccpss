Application Tips
  • Put your most desired course first, KUCCPS fills choices in order.
  • Include at least one KMTC or TVET programme as a safety backup.
  • Revision windows are limited, don't rely on them to fix a bad first selection.
  • Check minimum subject requirements before selecting a course.
  • HELB loans are tied to approved KUCCPS placements.
