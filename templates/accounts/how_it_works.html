Your Account

Register, sign in, and manage your profile.

1
Create Account — Register with your email

Click Create Account on the homepage or navigation bar. Enter your full name, email address, and a password. You will receive a verification email — click the link inside to activate your account. Registration is free and takes under 60 seconds.

2
Continue with Google — One-tap sign-in

On the Login page, click Continue with Google to sign in using your Google account. No password to remember — your Google account is linked to CareerNext automatically. Works on any device where you are already signed into Google.

3
Forgot Password — Reset your password

On the Login page, click Forgot password? and enter your email. A reset link is sent to your inbox. Click it, set a new password, and sign in immediately. Check your spam folder if you don't see the email within 2 minutes.

4
Edit Profile — Update your information

Go to My Profile from the top-right menu. Update your full name, county, KCSE year, and profile photo. This information helps the Career Engine give you more relevant recommendations and is never shared publicly.

5
Notifications — Stay informed

The bell icon in the top navigation shows unread notifications. Click it to see system alerts, updates to your shortlisted courses, mentorship confirmations, and important KUCCPS deadline reminders. Click any notification to go directly to the relevant page. Use Mark all as read to clear the badge.

You can browse courses and use the calculator without an account. Creating an account lets you save your results, build a shortlist, and get personalised recommendations.

Cluster Points Calculator

Enter your KCSE grades and get your cluster points for all 20 KUCCPS clusters.

1
Enter Grades Manually — Type each subject grade

The calculator shows all KCSE subjects grouped into sections (Core, Sciences, Humanities, etc.). For each subject you sat, select your grade from the dropdown — A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C, C-, D+, D, D-, E. Leave subjects blank if you did not sit them. The form updates your aggregate total in real time.

2
Upload Result Slip — OCR auto-fill

Click Upload Result Slip and take a clear, well-lit photo of your official KNEC result slip. Our AI (powered by GPT-4o Vision) reads the image and fills in all your grades automatically. Review the pre-filled grades for accuracy before calculating — blurry photos may need manual correction.

3
Calculate My Points — Run the formula

Click Calculate My Points after entering your grades. The system applies the official KUCCPS formula instantly and shows your cluster points for each of the 20 subject clusters (C1–C20), your KCSE aggregate out of 84, and your mean grade. The maximum cluster points score is 48.

4
View Eligible Courses — See what you qualify for

After calculating, click View Eligible Courses to see every university degree course whose cutoff point is at or below your calculated cluster points. The list updates in real time and shows all matching institutions and programmes across Kenya.

5
Export PDF — Download your results

Click Export PDF (or Download Report) on the results page to save a formatted PDF of your cluster points across all 20 clusters. Share it with parents, teachers, or keep it for your records. The PDF includes your aggregate, mean grade, and individual cluster scores.

6
Save My Results — Persist between sessions

Logged-in users see a Save My Results option. Saving stores your grades to your account so you don't have to re-enter them next time. Your saved results are used by the Dashboard and Career Engine to show personalised recommendations automatically.

How cluster points are calculated: The formula is 48 × √( (cluster_marks / 48) × (aggregate / 84) ). Your aggregate is Maths + best language (English or Kiswahili) + next 5 best subjects, capped at 84.

Eligible Courses

All courses whose cutoff is at or below your cluster points, with filters and actions.

1
Filter & Search — Narrow down the list

Use the filter bar at the top to narrow results by institution type (University, KMTC, TVET, TTC), cluster (e.g. Cluster 1 – Medicine), or county. Type in the search box to find a specific course or institution name. All filters combine — e.g. you can show only Nairobi universities in Cluster 3.

2
Bookmark — Save to Watchlist

Click the bookmark icon on any course card to save it to your Watchlist (Saved Courses). A blue bookmark means it's saved; click again to remove it. Access all your bookmarked courses from My Saved Courses in your Dashboard or account menu.

3
Add to Shortlist — Pick your top choices

Click Add to Shortlist (or the star icon) to move a course to your Shortlist — your curated list of top picks that mirrors the 4-choice KUCCPS application. From the Shortlist page you can rank, annotate, compare, and export your final picks.

4
View Details — Full course information

Click the course name or View Details to open the course detail page. Here you'll see the institution name, course duration, cluster requirements, historical cutoff points for 2021–2024, a cutoff trend chart, student reviews, and a link to the institution's website.

5
Recalculate — Change your grades

Click Recalculate at the top of the results page to go back to the calculator and adjust your grades. Useful if you want to explore a different grade scenario (e.g., "What if I got a B+ in Maths?").

Career Guidance Engine

A personalised engine that matches your grades and interests to the right courses and career paths.

1
Choose a Pathway — Degree, KMTC, TVET, or TTC

The engine starts by asking which education pathway interests you. Select one: Degree (university, cluster-points based), KMTC (medical training, grade-based), TVET (technical and vocational), or TTC (teacher training college). Each pathway uses different admission criteria, so the engine adapts accordingly.

2
Enter Your Grades — Or load saved results

Enter your KCSE grades on the engine's input form. If you have already calculated your points and saved them, click Load My Saved Grades to pre-fill the form automatically. For the Degree pathway, the engine calculates your cluster points in the background.

3
Proceed — Get your matches

Click Proceed to run the engine. It matches your grades against every course in its database and returns a personalised list of courses you qualify for, sorted by fit. You'll see degree options, KMTC programmes, TVET courses, or TTC programmes depending on your selected pathway.

4
AI Recommendation — CareerNext AI chat

After viewing your results, click Ask CareerNext AI or the AI chat button. This opens the CareerNext AI assistant — you can ask it questions like "Which of my eligible courses has the best job prospects?" or "Compare Nursing vs Clinical Medicine." The AI has full context of your grades and eligible courses.

5
Share My Results — Send to a parent or teacher

Click Share Results to generate a unique shareable link to your career engine results. Anyone with the link can view your course matches and AI recommendation — no login required. Links expire after 7 days for privacy.

6
Career Profiles — Explore career paths

Browse Career Profiles to learn about specific careers — what the job involves day-to-day, which courses lead to it, salary ranges, and growth prospects in Kenya. Tap Save Career on any profile to add it to your saved careers for future reference.

7
Career Quiz — Discover what suits you

Not sure what career to pursue? Click Take the Career Quiz. Answer 10–15 questions about your interests, strengths, and preferences. The quiz matches your personality to career profiles and shows the courses that lead there — a great starting point if you have no idea what to study.

Shortlist & Course Comparison

Build your final 4-choice list, rank it, annotate it, and compare courses side-by-side.

1
Add to Shortlist — From any course card

Click the star icon or Add to Shortlist button on any course. You can shortlist up to 4 courses — matching the 4 choices KUCCPS allows. The shortlist is accessible from the Dashboard and the top-right account menu.

2
Set Rank — Order your preference

On the Shortlist page, each course has a rank selector (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th choice). Set this to reflect your order of preference. Rank 1 is your top choice — KUCCPS places you into the highest-ranked course you qualify for.

3
Add Notes — Personal reminders

Click Add Note on any shortlisted course to write a personal annotation — e.g., "Mum prefers this," or "Check if UoN needs supplementary subjects." Notes are private and only visible to you.

4
Compare Courses — Side-by-side view

Click Compare All to open the course comparison table. This shows your shortlisted courses side-by-side with their cutoff points, cluster numbers, institution type, duration, and your margin above or below the cutoff — making it easy to spot the safest and most competitive choices.

5
Export Shortlist PDF — Print or share

Click Export PDF on the Shortlist page to download a formatted document showing all your shortlisted courses, their ranks, cutoff points, and your notes. Print it, share it with a parent, or show it to a school counsellor.

6
Remove from Shortlist — Change your mind

Click the remove icon (trash or × button) on any shortlisted course to take it off. You can add a different course in its place at any time.

KUCCPS allows 4 course choices per pathway. Use your shortlist to mirror this — rank carefully, with your safest option (lowest cutoff relative to your points) as choice 3 or 4.

Browse Courses & Institutions

Explore the full database of programmes at universities, KMTCs, TVETs, and TTCs.

1
Search — Find any course or institution

Use the search bar at the top of any browse page or the global search in the navigation. Type a course name (e.g., "Nursing"), an institution name (e.g., "Kenyatta"), or a cluster (e.g., "C1"). Results appear as you type — click any result to jump straight to its page.

2
Filter by Type / Cluster — Narrow your search

Filter courses by institution type (University, KMTC, TVET, TTC), cluster (C1–C20), county, or ownership (public / private / chartered). Filters stack — combine them to pinpoint exactly what you're looking for.

3
View Institution — See all courses at that school

Click any institution name to open its profile page. You'll see its type, location, accreditation status, website link, and a full list of all courses it offers with their cutoff points. Useful for students who have a preferred institution and want to see what it offers.

4
Leave a Review — Help other students

On a course detail page, logged-in users can leave a star rating and written review of a course or institution they have experience with. Reviews are visible to all students and help others make informed decisions.

5
Cutoff Trend Chart — See historical data

Each course detail page includes a cutoff trend chart showing how the cutoff point has changed from 2021 to 2024. A rising trend means the course is becoming more competitive; a falling trend may mean more seats or fewer applicants. Use this when comparing courses to judge how risky or safe each choice is.

Cutoff Predictor

Uses historical trends to estimate what the 2026 cutoff for each course might be.

1
Set Your Score — Enter your cluster points

At the top of the Predictor page, enter your cluster points (0–48). If you have already calculated your points, they will be pre-filled. The predictor uses this score to show your eligibility likelihood for each course — High Likelihood, Likely, Borderline, or Unlikely.

2
Filter by Eligibility — See only safe or borderline choices

Click the eligibility filter buttons — High Likelihood, Likely, Borderline, Unlikely — to show only courses in that band. Use Likely for safe choices and Borderline to find stretch goals within reach.

3
Trend Indicator — Rising, falling, or stable

Each course card shows a trend arrow — Rising (cutoff going up, harder to get in), Falling (cutoff dropping, easier), or Stable. A rising course means you should factor in extra margin when deciding.

4
Range Bar — See where you stand

Each course shows a range bar spanning the minimum to maximum cutoff seen historically. An orange tick marks your score on the bar — giving an instant visual of whether you're comfortably above the cutoff, right at the edge, or below it.

Predicted cutoffs are estimates based on past trends — not official figures. Actual 2026 cutoffs depend on the number of applicants and available slots. Use this as a guide, not a guarantee.

Mentorship

Book a 15-minute WhatsApp call with a real university or KMTC student. Honest answers, real insights.

1
Find a Mentor — Browse verified students

Go to Mentorship from the navigation. Browse the directory of verified current students. Filter by institution name, course, or year of study. Each mentor card shows their course, institution, rating, number of sessions done, and a short bio.

2
Book a Session — 15-minute WhatsApp call

Click Book a Session on any mentor's profile. Pick a date and time from their available slots, write a short message about what you want to discuss (optional), and confirm the booking. You'll be taken to the payment page immediately after.

3
Pay via M-Pesa STK Push — One tap payment

Enter your M-Pesa phone number and click Pay Now. A payment request is sent directly to your phone — open your M-Pesa app or enter your PIN when prompted. The session is confirmed within seconds once payment is received. No need to share MPesa statements.

4
Join the Session — Call your mentor on WhatsApp

After payment, you receive a WhatsApp link to your mentor. Message them to confirm the exact time or call directly at the agreed slot. Sessions are 15 minutes — come with your questions ready. Your mentor's WhatsApp number is shared only after payment.

5
Rate Your Session — Help the next student

After the session, you'll receive a prompt to rate your mentor (1–5 stars) and leave a short review. Ratings help other students choose the right mentor. Honest feedback is appreciated — it also helps mentors improve.

6
Become a Mentor — Earn while helping others

Are you a current university, KMTC, TVET, or TTC student? Click Become a Mentor to apply. Fill in your institution, course, year of study, and a short bio. Once verified by our team, your profile goes live in the directory. You earn a share of each session fee paid to you via M-Pesa.

Application Tracker

Track the status of each of your KUCCPS course applications in one place.

1
Add Application — Record your KUCCPS choice

Go to My Applications from the account menu or Dashboard. Click Add Application and fill in the course name, institution, KUCCPS choice number (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th), and current status. You can track up to 4 applications — one per KUCCPS choice.

2
Edit Status — Update as placement progresses

Click Edit on any tracked application to update its status — e.g., change from Submitted to Placed once KUCCPS releases results, or to Inter-institutional Transfer if you requested a change. Keeping this updated helps you stay organised during revision windows.

3
Delete — Remove an entry

Click Delete to remove a tracked application — for example, if you changed your mind about a choice before submission. Deletion is permanent, so confirm before proceeding.

The Application Tracker is a personal planning tool — it does not interact with KUCCPS. Your actual placement application must be submitted at students.kuccps.net during the official window.
