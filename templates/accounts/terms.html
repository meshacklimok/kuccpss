9. AI-Powered Features

9.1 Career Engine

Course recommendations on CareerNext are generated using our proprietary eligibility and matching system, which analyzes your KCSE grades, subject performance, and preferences against our course database. AI is only used within the Career Engine chat feature to provide additional guidance, explanations, and insights. AI-generated responses are for informational purposes only and may not always be accurate, complete, or up to date. Students should use them alongside official KUCCPS and institutional information when making decisions.

9.2 OCR Result Slip Reader

Uploading a photo of your Cluster weights sends that image to the OpenAI GPT-4o Vision API for text extraction. The image is not stored on CareerNext servers after processing. By using this feature, you consent to this transfer.
