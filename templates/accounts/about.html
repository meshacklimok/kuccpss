Our Mission

Demystifying the KUCCPS Process

The KUCCPS placement system is critical for every Form 4 leaver, yet many students are left guessing — unsure how cluster points are calculated, which courses they qualify for, or how to compare hundreds of options across universities, KMTCs, TVETs, and TTCs.

KUCCPSS was built to change that. We put the full KUCCPS formula into a simple tool, pair it with a complete course database, and wrap it in career guidance — so every student can walk into the application portal knowing exactly where they stand.

Whether you scored an A or a D+, there is a path for you. Our job is to help you find it.

How It Works

Four simple steps to find every course you qualify for across all KUCCPS institutions.

Important Distinction

KUCCPS vs KUCCPSS

Many students confuse the two. Here's the difference:

KUCCPS
Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service — the official government body that places students into institutions after KCSE. You apply at kuccps.ac.ke.
KUCCPSS (this site)
A free, independent platform that helps you prepare for KUCCPS — calculate your points, find qualifying courses, and plan your application. We are not affiliated with the government.
You still apply on KUCCPS
KUCCPSS helps you choose and prepare. Your actual placement application must be submitted on the official KUCCPS portal during the application window.
