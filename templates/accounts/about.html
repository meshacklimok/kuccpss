Our Mission

Demystifying the KUCCPS Process for Every Kenyan Student

The KUCCPS placement process is the gateway to higher education for hundreds of thousands of Kenyan students every year. Yet many Form 4 leavers enter it blind — unsure how cluster points are calculated, which courses they qualify for, or how to compare hundreds of options across universities, KMTCs, TVETs, and TTCs.

CareerNext was built to close that gap. We implement the official KUCCPS weighted formula in a transparent, student-friendly tool, back it with a complete and verified course database, and wrap it in career guidance so that every student — A grade or D+ — can make an informed choice.

Data Sources

Where Our Data Comes From

Transparency matters. Here is exactly where our course, cluster, and cutoff data comes from — and how we keep it accurate.

Primary
KUCCPS Official Publications

Cluster weights, subject group definitions, and cutoff points are sourced directly from official KUCCPS PDF releases and the kuccps.ac.ke portal each placement cycle.

Verified
Institutional Websites

Course names, durations, and admission requirements are cross-checked against official university, KMTC, TVET, and TTC websites to ensure accuracy.

Formula
Official Cluster Points Formula

The weighted cluster points formula we use mirrors the official KUCCPS computation: 48 × √((core/48) × (agg/84)). We do not approximate.

Ongoing
Annual Updates

Course data and cutoff points are reviewed and updated every KUCCPS placement cycle to reflect the latest approved programmes and capacities.

Community
User Corrections

Students and educators flag errors via our feedback form. Verified corrections are applied within 48 hours. Every correction improves the platform for all users.

Important Distinction

KUCCPS vs CareerNext

Many students confuse the two. Here is the clear difference:

KUCCPS
Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service — the official government body that places students into institutions after KCSE. You apply and receive placement at kuccps.ac.ke.
CareerNext (this site)
A fully accessible, independent preparation platform that helps you calculate your points, find qualifying courses, and plan your application. We are not affiliated with the government or KUCCPS.
Your placement application goes on KUCCPS
Use CareerNext to prepare and choose. Submit your actual placement application on the official KUCCPS portal during the application window.
